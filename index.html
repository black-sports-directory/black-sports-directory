    Get listed

    Listing is free. If you run a Black-led sports club, community group or sport business in the UK, or one that exists to get Black people into sport, we'd love to include you.

    who can be listed

    • Sports clubs and teams
    • Community groups and running, hiking or swimming crews
    • Charities and governing bodies
    • University and student sport groups
    • Activewear and sports brands
    • Physiotherapists, personal trainers and coaches

    How it works

    1. Fill in the form. It takes about five minutes.

    2. We check your details, usually within two weeks.

    3. Your listing goes live, and we'll let you know.

    Open the form

    Already listed? Use the same form to update your details or ask us to remove them.

    About us

    Black people in the UK are less likely to take part in many sports, and it's often hard to find clubs and groups where you feel at home. Black Sports Directory UK brings those organisations together in one place, so it's easier to find them, join them and support them.

    what we do

    • Keep a free, up-to-date directory of Black-led sport organisations
    • Share their stories through interviews and articles
    • Help more people find a sport and a community to do it with
